The first release of **The Aether Lite**, an unofficial cut-down fork of [The Aether](https://github.com/The-Aether-Team/The-Aether) for Fabric on Minecraft 26.3. It is not made or supported by The Aether Team, so please report problems on [this fork's issue tracker](https://github.com/stevelittlefish/The-Aether-Lite/issues).

## What's in it

- **The whole Aether adventure:** the portal, the dimension, its blocks, ores, tools, weapons, armour, mobs and Moas, and the Bronze, Silver and Gold dungeons with their bosses.
- **The vanilla inventory is left alone.** There are no accessory slots or accessory screen.
- **Capes** go in the chest slot, or onto any chestplate at a smithing table with no template needed.
- **Charms** work from the off hand: Shield of Repulsion, Golden Feather, Regeneration Stone, Iron Bubble, Zanite Ring and Ice Ring.
- **Removed:** gloves, pendants, the iron and gold rings, trivia, the Aether title screen, and the Patreon perks and chat message. Dungeon loot now gives other rewards in place of the removed items.

See the [README](https://github.com/stevelittlefish/The-Aether-Lite#readme) for the full list.

## Changes from the 26.2 Fabric port

- Ported to Minecraft 26.3.
- Flaming Sword fire and the bonus damage from the Holy Sword and Pig Slayer work again.
- The Zanite Sword's damage bonus from wear is back.
- Weapon lightning no longer strikes whoever is holding the weapon.
- Phoenix Bow arrows count as phoenix arrows again.
- Cloud Staff minions shoot when the staff is swung.
- Phyg and Flying Cow wings flap again.
- The Aether's loot modifiers, furnace fuels and compostables work again.
- The Icestone freezing recipe cache clears when resources reload.
- The Aether keeps its own time of day.
- Swets let go of you when they dissolve.
- Text that was drawn invisibly now shows: the Book of Lore, the Sun Altar, the travel message and the hammer's cooldown.

## Before you install

> [!WARNING]
> This version is **not save-compatible** with the full Aether mod. Worlds made with the full mod lose any removed items, including everything in the accessory slots.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- Fabric API
- Java 25

Optional: JEI, Jade, WTHIT, Diggus Maximus and Carry On.

Put `aether-lite-26.3-fabric-1.0.0.jar` in your `mods` folder. The `-sources` jar is the source code and isn't needed to play.
